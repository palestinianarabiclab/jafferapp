About me

"Specializing in teaching Palestinian dialect to complete beginners. I guide you step by step with patience and clarity until you feel confident using Arabic in real life."

A native Arabic speaker from Palestine-Gaza, I’m passionate about languages, culture, and helping people around the world communicate in Arabic with ease. I speak both Arabic and English, so I can guide you clearly—even if you're a complete beginner.

I have experience teaching Arabic to English-speaking students for absolute beginners.

I specialize in Palestinian dialect.

My lessons are interactive, personalized, and always focused on making you feel confident when you speak.

I also use visuals, examples from real-life situations, and clear English explanations to help you learn faster.

If you're thinking about learning Arabic, now is the perfect time to start!

I understand that learning a new language can be challenging, but with the right guidance, it becomes exciting and incredibly rewarding.

My goal is to ensure you feel confident, motivated, and comfortable in every lesson.

Together, we'll focus on what you need—whether it's fluency, understanding grammar, or simply enjoying the language.

You don't need any prior experience—just bring your curiosity, and I'll take care of the rest!

Book a trial lesson with me and let's begin your Arabic learning journey together.

I look forward to meeting you and helping you achieve your language goals!
